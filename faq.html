Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with abnerchinasourcing

How do I start sourcing products from China with abnerchinasourcing?

Simply fill out the inquiry form on our website with your product details, target quantity, and budget. We'll respond within 24 hours with a tailored proposal including supplier options and cost estimates.

What does a factory audit include?

Our factory audit covers facility inspection, production capacity assessment, quality management systems, certifications, social compliance, and worker welfare. You receive a detailed report with photos and video evidence.

How does your quality control inspection work?

We offer two-stage inspection: During Production (DUPRO) catches issues early, and Pre-Shipment Inspection (PSI) ensures final quality. Both follow AQL standards with detailed reporting.

What is landed cost analysis and why is it important?

Landed cost analysis calculates the total cost from factory to your doorstep, including product cost, shipping, duties, taxes, insurance, and handling fees. We identify hidden costs and negotiate better terms for you.

How do you ensure transparent pricing?

We provide 100% transparent pricing. You see exactly what the factory charges and what our service fee is. No hidden markups, no surprises.

Why work with a China sourcing agent instead of sourcing directly?

Sourcing from China directly involves significant risks that a professional procurement intermediary can mitigate:

  • Avoid Supplier Scams: We verify factory credentials, licenses, and capabilities to prevent fraud.
  • Overcome Language Barriers: Our bilingual team ensures clear communication of product specifications.
  • Save 15-30% on Costs: Leverage our supplier relationships and negotiation expertise for better pricing.
  • Quality Control: On-site inspections catch defects before shipment.
  • Legal Protection: We review contracts and Incoterms to protect your interests.
  • Save Time: We handle the entire process from factory search to shipping.
