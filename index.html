Our Services

Complete China Sourcing Solutions

From supplier selection to delivery — we cover every step of your procurement journey with transparency and professionalism.

Factory Audit & Inspection

工厂审核与实地验厂

Comprehensive on-site factory audits covering facilities, certifications, production capacity, and compliance. We verify every supplier so you can partner with confidence.

  • On-site facility & capability audit
  • License, compliance & social compliance
  • Detailed audit report with photos

Quality Control & Inspection

全流程质量控制与双重检验

Dual-stage quality checks — during production (DUPRO) and pre-shipment (PSI). Our experienced inspectors ensure your products meet every specification before leaving the factory.

  • During-production inspection (DUPRO)
  • Pre-shipment inspection (PSI)
  • Product testing & compliance certification

Landed Cost Analysis & Negotiation

到岸成本分析与询价谈判

Full cost transparency from factory to doorstep. We analyze every cost component and negotiate aggressively on your behalf to secure the best pricing and terms.

  • Payment terms & contract review
